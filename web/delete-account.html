Delete your account and profile data

You can request deletion without signing in. Enter the email address on your account and we will send a one-time confirmation link.

Send a deletion request

For your security, deletion starts only after you open the confirmation link sent to that email address.

What happens next

Data deleted

We delete your profile, photos and profile fields, interests, conversations and messages, reports, purchases, notifications, notification tokens, and your authentication account.

Data that may be retained

Only information required by law, fraud prevention, payment accounting, or unresolved safety investigations may be retained. It is restricted, securely stored, and deleted when the retention requirement ends.

How long it takes

After we receive and verify the request, account access is disabled immediately. Application data is normally deleted within 7 days. A legally required retention item may take longer, and we will explain the reason.

For help with a deletion request, contact support@alliancematrimony.in.
